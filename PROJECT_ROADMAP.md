# UNITER AI Education Platform

**Current Version:** v0.1.0

**Status:** Active Development

**Maintainer:** Timothy Chile

**Organization:** UNITER Inovar

---

# Vision

To build Africa's leading AI-powered education platform that provides personalized learning, intelligent tutoring, examination preparation, and classroom support for students, teachers, parents, and schools.

---

# Mission

Create an AI platform capable of teaching, explaining, assessing, and guiding learners from Junior Secondary School through University level.

The platform should eventually support:

* WAEC
* NECO
* JAMB
* BECE
* IGCSE
* GCSE
* Cambridge Curriculum
* University Courses
